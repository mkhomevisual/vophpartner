# VOPH Partners – Project Brief

## Positioning

VOPH Partners is a modern trading and sourcing partner focused on FMCG.

The company helps wholesalers, retail chains and large e-shops source products from leading global brands at competitive prices.

VOPH Partners is not a classic wholesaler and not an e-shop. It is a long-term partner connecting brands, logistics and markets.

## Website goals

- Generate new B2B leads.
- Generate enquiries.
- Build trust.
- Present the company professionally.
- Motivate visitors to call, email or schedule a meeting.

## Target customers

- Wholesalers.
- Retail chains.
- Large e-shops.
- Distributors.
- Large market players.

Communication is aimed at professionals, not end consumers.

## Customer value

- Better prices thanks to large volumes.
- FMCG know-how.
- Sourcing in Europe and abroad.
- Fast response.
- Simple cooperation.
- One contact point.
- Long-term partnership.

## Competitive advantages

VOPH Partners is a young company bringing a new approach to the established FMCG market.

Thanks to purchase volumes, it can often achieve better prices than customers can get directly.

It has strong logistics background and focuses on process efficiency, speed and simple cooperation.

## Brand character

- Modern.
- Practical.
- Minimalist.
- Reliable.
- Confident.
- International.
- Without unnecessary words.
- Long-term oriented.

If VOPH Partners were a person: an experienced trader. Professional, reliable, fast and long-term oriented. It does not sell at any cost; it builds relationships.

## Website style

- Modern.
- Fresh.

- Clean.
- Practical.
- Keep it simple.
- Large typography.
- A lot of space.
- Premium and professional look.

- Large typography.
- Minimal text.
- Subtle animations.
- Modern icons.

## Avoid

- Classic FMCG website.
- Generic WordPress template.
- Overcrowded design.
- AI-generated people.
- Handshake stock photos.
- Cheap stock photography.
- Too much text.
- Loud colors.
- Old trading-company look.

Prefer real photographs. Until real photos exist, use temporary direction or carefully selected non-cliché visual references.

Good themes:

- Logistics.
- Warehouses.
- Containers.
- Trucks.
- Ports.
- Pallets.
- Supply chain.
- FMCG products.
- Packaging details.

Avoid:

- Handshake photos.
- Artificial corporate style.
