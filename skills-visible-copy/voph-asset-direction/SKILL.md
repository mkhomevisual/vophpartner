# voph-asset-direction

## Required context

Before acting, read:

- `CLAUDE.md`
- `references/voph-project-brief.md`

If the repository has existing code, inspect it before editing.

## Non-negotiables

- The audience is B2B professionals, not consumers.
- The website is one-page unless instructed otherwise.
- The style is dark, premium, minimalist, spacious and modern.
- Copy must be short and confident.
- Do not invent contact data, certifications, legal claims or specific business relationships.
- Avoid AI-generated people, handshake imagery, cheap stock-photo style and generic FMCG web design.


## Visual asset direction

Prefer real or realistic visuals of:

- logistics,
- warehouses,
- containers,
- trucks,
- ports,
- pallets,
- supply chain,
- FMCG products,
- packaging details,
- abstract trade/network/logistics lines.

## Avoid

- people posing at camera,
- handshake photos,
- fake boardroom scenes,
- AI-generated people,
- cheap corporate stock,
- smiling manager clichés,
- images that make the company feel like an old trading firm.

## If real photos are unavailable

Use restrained abstract visuals:

- line networks,
- map-like routing,
- container-grid patterns,
- product-card silhouettes,
- barcode / pallet / supply chain motifs,
- monochrome or low-saturation imagery.

## Icon style

- thin or medium stroke,
- geometric,
- consistent corner radius,
- simple,
- no cartoon style.

## Output

Return asset recommendations per section, alt text, and implementation guidance.
